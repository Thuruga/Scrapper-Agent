<objective>
Add an explicit `sfcc`/`wake` guard to `EngineFactory.get_engine` (`backend/services/engines/factory.py`) so a brand registered with one of those engines fails clearly and diagnosably ("engine ... ainda não disponível") instead of silently falling through to `return VTEXEngine(brand_key)` at L45 (D-09, D-10).

Purpose: Phase 30 makes SFCC/Wake brands persist ACTIVE (SC-3), but their extraction engines do not ship until Phases 31/32. This closes the active-without-engine window: without the guard, every search/scan against an SFCC/Wake brand would silently run `VTEXEngine` against a non-VTEX domain (0 products / silent error each pass). The guard also fixes the latent fallback-to-VTEX that today swallows ANY unrecognized engine string.

Output: Modified `get_engine` — a guard for `engine_type in ("sfcc", "wake")` that raises `NotImplementedError` before the VTEX fallback, leaving every existing engine path intact.
</objective>
