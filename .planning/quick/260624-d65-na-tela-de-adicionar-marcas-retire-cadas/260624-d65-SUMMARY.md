# Quick Task 260624-d65 Summary

**One-liner:** Remove "Cadastrar Nova Marca" form from SettingsPage and reframe the screen as "Gerenciar Marcas" with full-width single-card layout.

## What Was Done

The brands screen (`SettingsPage` in `frontend/src/App.tsx`) was a two-column layout: a create form on the left and the brand list on the right. Per task requirements:

- The "Cadastrar Nova Marca" `<GlassCard>` (form with 3 inputs + submit button) was removed entirely.
- Dead-code cleaned up: `newBrand`/`setNewBrand`, `loading`/`setLoading`, `status`/`setStatus` state, and `handleSubmit` handler (the only caller of `ApiClient.saveBrand`).
- `handleToggleActive` error path was rewired from `setStatus(...)` to `toast.error(...)` — matching the established pattern throughout the file.
- The remaining card title was changed from "Marcas Cadastradas" to "Gerenciar Marcas".
- The `grid-2` wrapper div was removed; the single `<GlassCard>` now sits directly inside `<div className="page-content">` at full width.
- Page header fall-through title changed from `'Adicionar Marca'` to `'Gerenciar Marcas'`.
- Nav tab icon swapped from `PlusIcon` (plus sign — implies "add") to `Package` (already imported). The `Plus as PlusIcon` alias import was removed since no remaining references existed.
